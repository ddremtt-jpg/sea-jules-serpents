About Sea Jules Serpents

Quality-Focused. Carefully Selected.

Sea Jules Serpents is a small, quality-focused breeder specializing in exceptional locality boas, select morph boas, and carpet pythons. Our collection has been carefully assembled with an emphasis on quality, provenance, and established bloodlines.

We believe documentation matters. Lineage and provenance information is available to serious buyers upon request and applicable documentation is included with each purchase.

The Sea Jules Standard

Quality Comes First

We believe exceptional animals begin with careful selection and responsible breeding. Our goal is not to produce the most animals — it is to produce animals we are proud to put the Sea Jules name behind.

High-Quality Animals Our breeding stock is selected with an emphasis on health, appearance, structure, temperament, and long-term project value.
Documented Provenance Lineage and provenance information is maintained privately, available to serious buyers upon request, and applicable documentation is included with each purchase.
Selective Pairings Pairings are chosen deliberately to preserve important qualities and advance each project responsibly.
Direct Communication We prefer speaking directly with prospective buyers so we can discuss current animals, upcoming pairings, and the right fit for each keeper or breeder.
